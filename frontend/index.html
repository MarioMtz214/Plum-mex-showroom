About us

Passionate about Transforming Spaces

We are a husband-wife-run business and we genuinely care about customer satisfaction and our reputation. We are confident in the service that we provide and the premium fittings and furniture that we supply, and we are proud of the reputation we have built in the surrounding area to date. We believe in the importance of listening to our customers and understanding their needs and wishes, creating tailored and high-quality solutions. With an installation background himself, our director can guide customers on what is possible in the given space, as well as make innovative suggestions based on current trends or in keeping with the style of a house. We are passionate about transforming spaces and, with years of experience in the construction industry, we deliver exceptional results.

Services

We offer a wide range of services to meet all your
needs.

Installation

Plum-Mex has a team of hand-picked installers who we know and trust to get the job done professionally and efficiently. Our jobs span from simple improvements on existing kitchen and bathroom spaces to larger renovation projects which often require the movement of walls. From plasterers to electricians and carpenters to installers, we oversee every step of the process to ensure everything is coordinated and runs as smoothly as possible. We pride ourselves on the superior finish of our installations; our end goal is to not only meet but exceed our customers’ expectations.

Custom Design

At Plum-Mex, we believe that every project begins with a well-thought-out design. With an installer background himself, our expert designer listens to the customer’s requirements and makes suggestions on how to maximise the space’s efficiency and functionality whilst taking into consideration lifestyle, personal taste and the character of the home. From conceptualisation to final drawings, we ensure every detail is perfect to transform your ideas into reality.

Supply

We provide all the necessary materials for your project, collaborating with trusted suppliers to ensure only the highest quality materials are used. From flooring and furniture to taps and worktops, we offer a careful selection that meets your expectations and standards.
