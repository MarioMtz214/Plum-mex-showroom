About us

Passionate about Transforming Spaces

Whether it’s the replacement of a tired kitchen or bathroom, changing the lay-out or purpose of an existing space, or part of a larger extension project, we at Plum-Mex can help with our knowledge, best-in-class products, and quality workmanship. Services

We offer a wide range of services to meet all your needs

Supply:

offering an array of high-quality bathroom and kitchen displays

Our beautiful showroom helps you to visualise the possibilities for your new bathroom or kitchen with life-like displays from a variety of superior brands that span mid- to high-end budgets and provide solutions for traditional and modern homes alike. From furniture and tiles to brassware and glass, we offer a careful selection that meets your expectations.


“The showroom itself gave me the first indication of Benny’s attention to detail and his high standards” - Liam, Farnham

Design:

listening to your ideas and budget whilst offering expert advice

At Plum-Mex, we believe that every project begins with a well-thought-out design. With an installer background himself, Plum-Mex owner Benny has an immediate eye for how to maximise the efficiency of a given room or area. He takes time to visit your property and subsequently put together a comprehensive 3D design, taking into consideration your requirements and ideas whilst also making his own suggestions based on functionality, current trends and in keeping with the style of a house.


“Benny has a great creative mind offering up products and advice that will work best” - Charles Gregory, Odiham

Installation:

managing the entire project and ensuring a finish of the highest standard

We have a hard-working, friendly team of workers who we know and trust to get the work done professionally and efficiently. From plasterers to electricians, carpenters to installers, we oversee every step of the process to ensure everything is coordinated and that any issues that arise are resolved quickly. We pride ourselves on the superior finish of our renovations; our end goal is to not only meet but exceed your expectations.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to visit the showroom before starting a project? +

While it’s not essential, we strongly recommend visiting our Crondall showroom. Seeing kitchen and bathroom displays in person helps you compare finishes and layouts and allows us to understand your preferences from the start.

Which areas do you cover? +

Our showroom is based in Crondall (GU10 5RX), ideally located between Farnham and Odiham. We regularly work with clients across Farnham, Odiham, Fleet and surrounding areas of Hampshire and Surrey.

Do you offer design, supply and installation as one service? +

Yes. We provide a complete service covering design, product supply and full installation, ensuring a smooth and well-managed project from start to finish.

Can you work with my budget? +

We work with a wide range of budgets and always recommend the most suitable options within your price range, without compromising on quality.

How do I get started? +

Simply visit our showroom or contact us to arrange an initial consultation. We’ll guide you through the next steps based on your needs.
