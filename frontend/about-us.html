About Plum-Mex Showroom

Transforming kitchens and bathrooms across Crondall, Farnham, Odiham, and nearby areas.

At Plum-Mex Showroom, we transform your ideas into reality. From the initial design to the final installation of your new kitchen or bathroom, we provide tailored solutions that reflect your vision and enhance your lifestyle. Located in Crondall, just off the A287 between Farnham and Odiham, we proudly serve customers within a 20–30 km radius including Fleet, Alton, Aldershot, Hook, and Basingstoke.

As a family-run business, we care deeply about customer satisfaction and our reputation in the local community. With years of experience in the construction and installation industry, our team ensures that every project—big or small—is completed to the highest standard. From premium fittings to bespoke furniture, we use only the finest materials to create timeless, functional spaces.

Our services include:

  • Custom Design – Expertly planned layouts that maximise space and reflect your personal style.
  • Supply – High-quality materials and furniture from trusted manufacturers.
  • Installation – A professional, coordinated service from start to finish, managed by our trusted team of installers.

Whether you’re looking to redesign a modern kitchen in Farnham or install a luxury bathroom in Odiham, Plum-Mex Showroom is your trusted local partner. Visit us at our Crondall showroom to discover how we can bring your ideas to life.
